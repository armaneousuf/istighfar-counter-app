App Guide & Information

Counting
Tap the big circle to count each Istighfar. Use Undo if you tap by mistake, and Reset Session to clear the current count without losing your daily total.
Setting a Goal
Choose a preset goal (100 / 1,000 / 3,000 / 10,000) or set a custom number. The ring around the counter fills as you progress. Completing your goal triggers a celebration. 🎉
Daily Streaks
Your streak grows by 1 for every consecutive day you use the app. Opening the app on any new day automatically archives yesterday's count to your activity history.
Focus Mode
Tap the ⛶ expand button in the top bar to enter full-screen Focus Mode. Tap anywhere on the screen to count — perfect for hands-free use during prayer.
Ranks & Badges
Earn 15 ranks (★1–★15) as your lifetime total grows. Unlock 15 milestone badges at key counts (33, 100 … 10,000,000). Tap the button to see all achievements.
Backup & Restore
Use Export to save your data as a .json file (shared via your phone's share sheet on Android). Use Import to restore from a backup on a new device.
Intention (Niyyah)
Do not count merely to increase numbers. This app is designed to help you track your goals and stay motivated in remembering Allah. Let your primary intention be to please Allah, not just to reach a high count. May Allah accept our efforts.
